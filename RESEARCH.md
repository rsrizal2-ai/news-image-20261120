# Research notes — world news, Friday 20 November 2026

All stories below happened, or were first reported, on 20 November 2026
(UTC) or late on 19 November in time zones where the day had already
turned. Sources checked on 20 November 2026 (WITA morning).

## The six stories

### 1. Shenzhen — first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit
On 19 November, at Kylin Villa in Shenzhen, the presidents of the US,
China and Russia held their first-ever three-way meeting, on the
sidelines of the APEC summit. They adopted a joint declaration on
peace, covering Ukraine, Iran, trade, energy and nuclear arms control;
Trump renewed his call for an arms agreement including China. US peace
envoy Steve Witkoff said Trump made resolving the Ukraine conflict a
priority at the summit.
Sources: TASS via stockbiz.vn; Reuters/DW via daily8news.com;
logichubdx.blogspot.com.

### 2. The Hague — ICJ: Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya
On 19 November the International Court of Justice ruled, by 9 votes to
6, in The Gambia v. Myanmar, that Myanmar committed genocide against
the Rohingya during the 2016–17 "clearance operations" in Rakhine
State. It is the first ICJ genocide verdict against a state in years.
Sources: logichubdx.blogspot.com; New York Times via
srihayavadhana.com ("U.N. Court Rules Myanmar Committed Genocide
Against Rohingya").

### 3. Wall Street — Anthropic's Nasdaq debut soars
Anthropic began trading on Nasdaq under ticker ANT on 19 November,
after pricing 825 million Class A shares at $70–78. Shares closed up
17% on the first day, around $97, valuing the company at about
$1.76tn — the first purely AI company to list publicly.
Sources: AFP via stockbiz.vn; Times of India via srihayavadhana.com;
daily8news.com.

### 4. Riyadh — Houthis claim strikes on four Saudi targets
On 19 November, Houthi forces in Yemen said they had carried out
drone and missile attacks on four targets in Saudi Arabia and warned
foreign investors to leave the kingdom: a financial centre, King
Khalid International Airport in Riyadh, the Sadaf petrochemical
company in Jubail, and an air base in Khamis Mushait.
Sources: TASS via stockbiz.vn; Daily Baku (dailybaku.az).

### 5. Kyiv — four ballistic missiles hit the capital
Kyiv was hit by four ballistic missiles overnight on 19–20 November,
as European allies of Ukraine said they were ready for talks with
Russia on ending the war.
Sources: Daily Baku (dailybaku.az, 04:07, 20 Nov 2026);
jabulaniradio.com.

### 6. Bay of Bengal — Cyclone Akvan nears India's coast
Severe Cyclonic Storm Akvan, over the west-central Bay of Bengal, was
due to cross the North Andhra Pradesh and South Odisha coasts close to
Gopalpur by noon on Friday 20 November, with sustained winds of
90–100 kmph gusting to 110. Odisha evacuated more than 80,000 people
to safety (rising towards 300,000 by Thursday evening), deployed
hundreds of rescue teams and cancelled trains.
Sources: India Meteorological Department via thedailyjagran.com;
The Hindu BusinessLine ("Odisha evacuates over 80,000 people,
deploys 74 rescue teams in view of Cyclone Akvan").
